Welcome to the Zenith Demo

This page demonstrates a dynamic navbar that also serves as the hero section. Watch the space above as you scroll down. The section will shrink only after you cross the 10vh scroll threshold, then it becomes fixed/sticky.

Section One: Adaptive Design

Modern web design thrives on adaptability. The viewport height (vh) is calculated dynamically, ensuring the shrinking threshold is always 10% of the user's screen height, regardless of device size. This guarantees the intended user experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Section Two: The Sticky Transition

The use of CSS transitions combined with JavaScript class toggling provides a buttery-smooth effect. Instead of an abrupt change, the height and font size animate gracefully, leading to a much more polished and engaging visual experience. Notice how the large title becomes a concise header upon scrolling.

Section Three: Ensuring Readability

When the navigation shrinks, its purpose shifts from a dramatic hero banner to a functional, persistent tool. The title is resized to maintain clarity without dominating the page, and the fixed position ensures access to navigation elements (if they were present) at all times.

Scroll further down to confirm the sticky behavior persists. This large block of content ensures you have plenty of scrolling space to test the feature effectively and confirm the sticky nav remains at the top. The smooth transition class also prevents content from "jumping" when the navbar switches from relative to fixed positioning.
